🔒 SafeVault - Keep your private passwords secure offline

Download SafeVault for Windows

SafeVault helps you manage logins and passwords without sending your data to the internet. Your information stays on your computer. We use high-level encryption to lock your data, so only you hold the keys.

✨ Why use SafeVault

Modern password management requires trust. Many services store your data on their servers. SafeVault differs because it operates as an offline-first application. No data ever leaves your device.

  • Zero-Knowledge: We have no access to your credentials. Your master password decrypts your vault locally.
  • Offline Access: You do not need a connection to look up your passwords.
  • Windows Ready: SafeVault runs natively on Windows 10 and Windows 11.

📥 Download and Installation

Follow these steps to install SafeVault on your computer. You do not need any coding knowledge to complete this setup.

  1. Visit the project download page.
  2. Look for the latest release version on the page.
  3. Click the file ending in .exe to start the download.
  4. Open the installer file once the download finishes.
  5. Follow the screen prompts to finish the installation.
  6. Find the SafeVault icon on your desktop or start menu to begin.

If Windows shows a security prompt during installation, click "More info" and then "Run anyway." This happens because we provide the software directly to you without third-party app store mediation.

🛡️ Security Features

SafeVault uses industry-standard encryption. We rely on AES-GCM, which provides both secrecy and integrity for your stored data. This means your vault cannot be opened or tampered with by outside parties. Because the app works offline, your master password travels nowhere. It exists only in your computer's memory while the vault remains unlocked.

⚙️ System Requirements

SafeVault works on any modern Windows machine. Ensure your system meets these points:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • At least 200MB of free disk space.
  • 4GB of RAM for smooth operation.
